Project overview

Featured project

F1 News Hub

I built F1 News Hub to bring headlines from different Formula 1 news sites into one place. You can search for a story or filter by source, then head to the original site to read it.

Built with
Next.js and Tailwind CSS.
How it works
It collects the headlines and links back to the publishers. The full articles stay on their own sites.
  • Next.js
  • Tailwind CSS

A bit about me

I’m an implementation consultant at Dotdigital. I help clients set up personalisation, working out what they want to do and getting it working with JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

I started at Fresh Relevance in customer support and later led the Professional Services team before moving into consulting. I’ve spent a lot of time helping people figure out the technology as well as working on the code.

Outside work, I like having a side project on the go. I tend to learn by trying things out and figuring them out along the way.

I also dabble with Raspberry Pi, ESP32 boards, and random sensor kits. Mostly just tinkering and seeing what I can get working.
