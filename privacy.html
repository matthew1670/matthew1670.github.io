Privacy & analytics

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This is my personal website. You don’t need an account to browse it, and analytics is optional.

If you email me

The email link opens your email app. If you send me a message, I’ll receive your email address and anything you include in it so I can reply. You can email hello@matthewwood.me if you have any questions about your information.

Optional analytics

The homepage and this page only load Google Analytics if you enable it below. It helps me see how people use these pages. When enabled, it sends usage information to Google and may store cookies in your browser. Read Google’s privacy policy to find out how Google handles that information.

Your browser remembers your choice using local storage. You can turn analytics off here at any time. This stops future analytics on these pages and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site; it does not delete information already sent to Google. Clearing browser storage resets your preference to off.

Your analytics choice

Analytics is off unless you’ve already turned it on in this browser.

Hosting and other websites

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information needed to serve pages, such as IP addresses. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Links to projects, GitHub, LinkedIn, and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.
